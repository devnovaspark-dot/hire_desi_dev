WEBSITE BUILD BRIEF

Hire AI Developer — the website

A premium, conversion-first site that turns international visits into AI developer requirements. This brief covers the pages, the design direction, the form and the launch plan, with mock-ups on the boards beside it.

01 · WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

One offer, one action: submit a requirement

Hire AI Developer supplies vetted AI developers to international clients. The site has a single job: get a visitor to describe their requirement in a short form, so our team can match the right developer. Everything on the page supports that one action.

02 · NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

No pricing, anywhere on the site

We never show prices or hourly fees. Visitors see the three ways to hire, by name only. Commercials are discussed after the requirement is reviewed, not on the website.
